# Reports

- `decisions_summary.json`: underwriting decisions for the 16 fixture merchants
  (`make screen`). It does not depend on the workbench.
- `monitoring_alerts.json` and `monitoring_memos.md`: the portfolio monitor's alerts
  and the memos drafted for them. Both were produced on the synthetic world of
  [`bnpl-fraud-workbench`](https://github.com/nacapule/bnpl-fraud-workbench/tree/v1-2026-08)
  at tag `v1-2026-08` (seed 416, orders from 2025-07-01 to 2026-06-25). Merchant ids
  and dates refer to that world.
